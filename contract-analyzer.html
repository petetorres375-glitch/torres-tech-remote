Vendor Services Agreement
High risk
✓Payment terms: Net 30 — standard and acceptable.

How it works

Three steps, under a minute.

  1. Upload the contract

    A PDF, Word (.docx), Pages or TXT file — or take photos of a paper contract with your phone.

  2. Let it read

    The analyzer reads the contract and sticks to its exact wording, quoting the key phrases where they matter.

  3. Review before you sign

    Read the red flags and the recommendation, then decide what to ask about, negotiate or take to a lawyer.

ⓘ Best for contracts up to about 5 pages. A sheet printed on both sides counts as 2 pages. With photos, take one per side, up to 6. On longer contracts, only about the first 5 pages are analyzed.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is this legal advice?
No. The Contract Analyzer gives you a plain-language summary, a risk assessment and the key clauses so you understand what you're signing. It's a starting point, not a substitute for a lawyer — for a high-stakes contract, use the red flags as your list of questions for one.
What files can I upload?
PDF, Word (.docx), Apple Pages and TXT files. Scanned contracts work too, and you can photograph a paper contract with your phone — one photo per side, up to 6 photos.
How long a contract can it handle?
About 5 pages. A sheet printed on both sides counts as 2 pages. On a longer contract only about the first 5 pages are analyzed, and the app tells you so.
Do you store my contract?
No. Your contract is processed to produce your result and nothing is kept afterward. See the Privacy Policy.
