FAQ

Common questions.

What is an ATS?
An applicant tracking system is software many employers use to collect and sort resumes. It reads your resume as text and looks for the skills and words that match the job, so a resume missing those words can be passed over before a person sees it.
Does a high score guarantee an interview?
No. Every employer's system and every hiring manager is different. The score shows how well your resume covers what screening systems commonly look for, and the recommendations show how to improve it.
Can I paste in a specific job posting?
Not in the ATS Analyzer. It checks your resume against one of 42 built-in jobs, or a general check if you don't pick one. Comparing several resumes against a full job description is a Workblox Business tool, the Batch ATS Analyzer.
What files and languages work?
PDF, Word (.docx), Apple Pages and TXT files up to 5 MB. Resumes written in English or Spanish can be scored. The app itself runs in 24 languages.
Do you store my resume?
No. Your resume is read to produce your score and nothing is kept afterward. See the Privacy Policy.
