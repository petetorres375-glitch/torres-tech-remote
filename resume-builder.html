How it works

    Your name, email, phone, city, LinkedIn and the job you're aiming for.

    Degrees, diplomas or certificates, with the school and year.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need to know how to write a resume?
No. Describe what you did at each job in plain words, the way you'd tell a friend. The Resume Builder does the wording and the layout.
Will it make things up?
It's instructed to polish only what you give it, never to invent jobs, schools or numbers. As with anything AI writes, read your resume over before you send it — it's your name on it.
What can I download?
A PDF ready to send, a Word file you can edit, or plain text to paste into online application forms.
What languages does it write in?
The app runs in 24 languages, and your resume is written in the language your app is set to.
Do you store my information?
No. What you enter is used to write your resume and isn't kept afterward, so download your copy before you leave. See the Privacy Policy.
