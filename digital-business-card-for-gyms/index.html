Put it on a phone in under a minute

Two quick things your members can do, on iPhone or Android.

iPhone

Add it to the home screen, so it opens like an app

  1. Open Safari and type in your card’s link.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to home screen on the card.
  3. Tap Share (the square with an arrow).
  4. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Save the contact

  1. Open Safari and type in your card’s link.
  2. Scroll down and tap Save contact on the card.
  3. When the contact opens, tap the check mark at the top left.

Android

Add it to the home screen, so it opens like an app

  1. Open the Chrome browser and type in your card’s link.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to home screen on the card.
  3. Tap Install.
  4. The card’s icon is added to your home screen and to your app list.

Save the contact

  1. Open your card.
  2. Tap the Save contact button.
  3. Tap Download in the box that shows the file name (like torres-tech-remote.vcf) and where it will be saved.
  4. Open the downloaded file.
  5. On the next screen, tap Import and choose where to save it.
  6. The contact is now in your contacts list.

Not sure how? We email you these steps to share with your members, and we can walk you through them by phone or video call, from anywhere in the U.S.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get gym walk-ins to book a class?
Give them one easy next step. A QR code at your front desk or on your flyer opens your card, and one tap opens your own booking page, calls or texts you, or saves your contact. The card doesn't replace your booking system, it gets people to it faster.
How do I stop clients from losing my number?
Ask them to tap Save contact on your card. Your gym goes straight into their phone's contacts, so they don't need a paper card or your Instagram handle.
What is a digital business card?
It is a short web page built for phones. People open it with a link or a QR code, nothing is downloaded, and it can be added to the home screen like an app.
How much does it cost?
Design and setup are free. After that it is $10 a month or $100 a year. You see a preview before you pay.
Can it use the booking app my gym already has?
Yes. The main button can open your booking, class or membership page. The card does not replace that system, it sends people to it.
How do my members put it on their phone?
On an iPhone or an Android phone, they can add the card to the home screen so it opens like an app, and save your contact in one tap. We email you simple step-by-step instructions for iPhone and Android to share with your members, and we can walk you through it by phone or video call.
Can my coaches and trainers have their own cards?
Yes. Each coach or trainer can get their own card at the same price as yours, and they order it themselves. We can also make one card per location. For several locations or a team card we give you a custom quote, and the business pays for all the cards.
Who makes the changes?
We do, like a concierge. Send us an email with what to change, such as a class time, a price, a photo or a promotion, and we update it.
Which languages does it support?
We offer 25 languages, including Spanish, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese and Arabic. Each card shows up to 3 of them, and each visitor sees the one that matches their phone's language.
