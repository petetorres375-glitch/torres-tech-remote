Put it on a phone in under a minute

Two quick things your members can do, on iPhone or Android.

Add it to the home screen, so it opens like an app

iPhone. Open the card in Safari. Tap Share (the square with an arrow). Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android. Open the card in Chrome. Tap Add to home screen, then Install.

Save the contact

iPhone. Tap Save contact on the card. When the contact opens, tap the check mark at the top left.

Android. Tap Save contact. When it downloads, tap Open, then Import (some phones say Save).

Not sure how? We email you these steps to share with your members, and we can walk you through them by phone or video call, from anywhere in the U.S.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get gym walk-ins to book a class?

Give them one easy next step. A QR code at your front desk or on your flyer opens your card, and one tap opens your own booking page, calls or texts you, or saves your contact. The card doesn't replace your booking system, it gets people to it faster.

How do I stop clients from losing my number?

Ask them to tap Save contact on your card. Your gym goes straight into their phone's contacts, so they don't need a paper card or your Instagram handle.

What is a digital business card?

It is a short web page built for phones. People open it with a link or a QR code, nothing is downloaded, and it can be added to the home screen like an app.
