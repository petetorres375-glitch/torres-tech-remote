Works like an app

Your card on their home screen.

Customers save your card to their phone in a few taps. It gets its own icon, opens full screen like an app, and still opens without internet. No app store and no download. Pick your phone to watch how:

Android

  1. Open the card in Chrome.
  2. Tap Add to home screen on the card.
  3. Tap Install.

iPhone

  1. Open the card in Safari (iPhone or iPad).
  2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Saved in their contacts

In their contacts in seconds.

“Save contact” adds your business to their contacts with your logo, phone, email, address and website, so they can find you and call you later. Pick your phone to watch how:

Android

  1. Tap Save contact on the card.
  2. It downloads. Tap Open.
  3. Tap Import (some phones say Save).

iPhone

  1. Tap Save contact on the card.
  2. The contact opens. Tap ✓ at the top left.

FAQ

Common questions.

What does it cost?
Design and setup are free. You pay $10 per month for hosting and updates, or $100 per year (2 months free). Up to 3 free previews before you pay. For several locations or employees, request a custom quote here.
Which languages can my card show?
Up to 3 of 25 languages, like Spanish, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese and Arabic. See the full list.
Do my customers need to download an app?
No. The card opens from its link or QR code. Customers can also save it to their home screen like an app, no app store. On Android, open it in Chrome and tap Add to home screen, then Install. On iPhone or iPad, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. See the steps.
Can customers book, order or get a quote from my card?
Yes. Your card’s main button, like Book now, Order online or Get a quote, opens the booking, ordering or quote page you already use, such as Square, Booksy, OpenTable or DoorDash. Or it can call, text or WhatsApp you. It works for barbers and salons, cafés and restaurants, contractors, pest control and more.
Can I change my hours or prices later?
Yes. Hosting includes unlimited changes to your hours, prices, photos and links. Your QR code never changes, so printed codes keep working.
How do I pay?
Card accepted online and in person. Cash in person for yearly plans. After you approve your free preview, pay online with the payment links.
Can one card cover several locations?
Yes. One card lists every location, each with its own address, directions, phone number and hours. Each location can also have its own QR code, so a customer who scans it at that location lands on that location. See the examples.
Can each of my employees have their own card?
Yes. Each employee receives an individual card with their name, photo and contact details. One office card lists the entire team, and each person can be assigned to the location where they work.
How much does it cost for several locations or employees?
Each business is different, so we provide a custom quote based on your number of locations and employees. Setup is free. Request a quote.
What happens when we add or lose an employee?
You can add people at any time and they are billed for the rest of that year. If someone leaves, you can give their place to a new person at no charge. Please tell us by the 1st of the month, and we make the change that week.
What if one of my locations is a franchise?
A location owned by another business is a separate customer with its own card and its own bill. It can link back to your main card if you both agree.
Who can order a card?
In-person service in the Ironbound, Newark NJ; online orders available in the US and its territories, and to US citizens abroad paying with a US card or bank account.
