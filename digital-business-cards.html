FAQ

Common questions.

What does it cost?
$100 one time for design and setup, then $10 per month for hosting and updates, or $100 per year (2 months free). Up to 3 free previews before you pay.
Which languages can my card show?
Up to 3 of 25 languages, like Spanish, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese and Arabic. See the full list.
Do my customers need to download an app?
No. The card opens from its link or QR code, and installs on customers’ phones like an app, no app store.
Can customers book, order or get a quote from my card?
Yes. Your card’s main button, like Book now, Order online or Get a quote, opens the booking, ordering or quote page you already use, such as Square, Booksy, OpenTable or DoorDash. Or it can call, text or WhatsApp you. It works for barbers and salons, cafés and restaurants, contractors, pest control and more.
Can I change my hours or prices later?
Yes. Hosting includes unlimited changes to your hours, prices, photos and links. Your QR code never changes, so printed codes keep working.
How do I pay?
Card accepted online and in person. Cash in person for setup and yearly plans. After you approve your free preview, pay online with the payment links.
Who can order a card?
In-person service in the Ironbound, Newark NJ; online orders available in the US and its territories, and to US citizens abroad paying with a US card or bank account.
